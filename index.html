Website kaise kharidein?

Sabse pehle login ya sign up karein (bina login ke kharidari nahi hoti). Phir apni pasand ki website par BUY NOW dabayein, ya Add to Cart karke cart se checkout karein. Phir payment page par QR scan karke UPI se payment karein.

Payment kaise karni hai?

Payment page par diya gaya QR code apne UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm etc.) se scan karein, payment karein, phir "I Have Paid" button dabayein.

Payment ke baad kya hoga?

"I Have Paid" dabane ke baad hamari team aapki payment check karti hai aur aapse sampark karti hai.

Kya main apne hisaab se alag website bana sakta hoon?

Ji haan. "Contact Us" par click karke form bharein aur apni requirement likhein, hum aapse baat karenge.

Delivery, support ya aur kisi cheez ke baare mein poochna hai?

Contact form bharein ya WhatsApp button se seedha message karein, hamari team aapko sahi jankari degi.
